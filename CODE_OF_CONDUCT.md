# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Remembering that not everyone speaks English — or Portuguese — as a first
  language, and that an abrupt-sounding message is usually just a translation

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair
corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate. They may
remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour through GitHub's
private reporting on this repository. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated, and will result in a response appropriate to the circumstances.
The reporter's privacy and safety are respected.
